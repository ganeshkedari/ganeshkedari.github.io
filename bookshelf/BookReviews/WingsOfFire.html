Wings of Fire

Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

4.7
Inspirational

"Dream is not that which you see while sleeping, it is something that does not let you sleep."

Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

Wings of Fire is the inspiring autobiography of Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam, India's beloved Missile Man and former President. The book traces his journey from a humble childhood in Rameswaram to becoming one of India's most respected scientists and leaders. Dr. Kalam shares his experiences working on India's space and missile programs, including the development of the SLV-3 and the Agni and Prithvi missiles. Written with Arun Tiwari, the narrative is filled with anecdotes about perseverance, dedication, and the power of dreams. Dr. Kalam's simple yet profound writing style makes this a deeply personal account of a life dedicated to science and nation building.

This book is a treasure for anyone who wants to understand what true dedication looks like. Dr. Kalam writes with such honesty and humility that you feel like he is sitting across from you, sharing his life story over tea. What struck me most was how he never gave up, even when projects failed or when he faced setbacks. His love for India and his belief in young people is infectious. The book is not preachy at all. It simply shows through real examples how hard work, integrity, and a clear vision can take you anywhere. Every Indian should read this at least once.
