The Power of Your Subconscious Mind

Joseph Murphy

4.3
Powerful

"Whatever your conscious mind assumes and believes to be true, your subconscious mind will accept and bring to pass."

Joseph Murphy

The Power of Your Subconscious Mind teaches you how to harness the untapped power of your subconscious to transform your life. Joseph Murphy, a spiritual teacher and New Thought minister, explains that your subconscious mind is like a powerful computer that can be programmed through affirmations, visualization, and positive thinking. The book covers topics ranging from healing and health to wealth and relationships, all through the lens of mind power. Murphy draws on religious texts, scientific principles, and countless personal testimonies to illustrate how the subconscious works. First published in 1963, it remains a classic in the self-help genre, introducing techniques that have inspired modern concepts like the law of attraction.

This book introduced me to the idea that our thoughts really do shape our reality. Murphy writes in a very optimistic and encouraging way, which can feel repetitive at times but also drives the message home. Some of the examples feel a bit dated, but the core principles still work if you apply them consistently. I found the techniques like autosuggestion before sleep really helpful during exam preparations. The book has a spiritual tone, which some might find too religious, but I think it fits well with Indian philosophical ideas about the power of the mind. It is a good starting point for anyone interested in positive thinking and self-improvement.
