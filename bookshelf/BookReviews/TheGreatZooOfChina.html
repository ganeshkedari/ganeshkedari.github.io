The Great Zoo of China

Matthew Reilly

4.4
Non-Stop Action

"Welcome to the world's most spectacular theme park. Where dragons are real."

Matthew Reilly, The Great Zoo of China

The Great Zoo of China is an adrenaline-fueled thriller by Australian author Matthew Reilly, published in 2014. The story begins when reptile expert Dr. Cassandra Jane "CJ" Cameron is invited to a top-secret Chinese facility to witness the opening of an extraordinary new zoo that houses real, living dragons. Matthew Reilly is known for his fast-paced, cinema-ready action novels filled with larger-than-life scenarios and relentless thrills. When things inevitably go wrong and the dragons escape, what follows is a desperate fight for survival in a massive underground complex. Reilly combines Jurassic Park-style creature feature with his trademark explosive action sequences, creating a book that reads like a blockbuster movie. The novel explores different species of dragons, each with unique abilities, all backed by pseudo-scientific explanations that make the impossible feel plausible. With detailed diagrams, maps, and breakneck pacing, Reilly delivers pure entertainment that never slows down.

This book is absolutely insane in the best way possible. From the moment the dragons escape, it is one action scene after another with barely any time to breathe. Reilly writes action better than almost anyone, making you see every explosion, every chase, and every narrow escape like you are watching it on screen. The dragons are terrifying and awesome at the same time, each type bringing different challenges. What makes it fun is that Reilly knows this is over the top and embraces it fully without trying to be too serious. CJ is a solid protagonist who uses her brain as much as her reflexes to survive. The book includes maps and diagrams which really help visualize the massive underground zoo. Some character deaths happen so quickly you barely process them, but that adds to the chaos. If you want smart literary fiction, look elsewhere. But if you want pure, crazy, dragon-filled action that makes hours disappear, this is perfect.
