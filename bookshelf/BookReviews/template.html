“इतिहासातला खलनायक म्हणवला गेलेला रावण, खरंतर एका युगाचा तेजस्वी, विद्वान आणि संघर्षमय राजा होता.”

Ravan Raja Rakshanscha by Sharad Tandale is a bold and compelling retelling of the life of Ravana — the most controversial figure of the Ramayana. Tandale presents Ravana not merely as a demon king but as a powerful administrator, an exceptional scholar, a Shiva devotee, and a visionary ruler of Lanka.

Through detailed research, emotional depth, and a narrative rooted in Marathi cultural tone, the book humanizes Ravana’s journey, his rise, his love, his policies, and ultimately his tragic downfall.

Sharad Tandale’s writing stands out for its grounded realism and balanced portrayal. Instead of glorification or one-sided blame, the author presents a layered character shaped by ambition, devotion, pride, and pain.
