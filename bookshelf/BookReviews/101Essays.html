101 Essays That Will Change the Way You Think

Brianna Wiest

4.4
Thought-provoking

"You don't need to change your life. You need to change your relationship with yourself and, in turn, your life will change."

Brianna Wiest

101 Essays That Will Change the Way You Think is a collection of powerful and provocative essays on self-awareness, personal growth, and living an authentic life. Brianna Wiest, a popular writer and poet, compiles some of her most impactful pieces originally published online that have resonated with millions. The essays tackle topics ranging from emotional intelligence and mindfulness to relationships and finding meaning in suffering. Each piece is short but dense with insights, designed to make you pause and reconsider your assumptions. Wiest writes with a raw honesty that cuts through the noise of typical self-help advice, offering a fresh perspective on how to navigate modern life's complexities.

This book is like having deep conversations with a wise friend who is not afraid to tell you the truth. I loved how each essay is short enough to read during a tea break but deep enough to think about for days. Some essays hit really hard, especially the ones about emotional baggage and self-sabotage. Wiest does not sugarcoat things or offer easy solutions. Instead, she makes you question your own patterns and beliefs. I found myself highlighting almost every page. The writing feels personal and relatable, not preachy at all. It is the kind of book you can open to any random page and find something useful. Perfect for anyone who wants to understand themselves better.
