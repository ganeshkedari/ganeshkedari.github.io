Siddhartha

Hermann Hesse

“Wisdom cannot be passed on. Wisdom that a wise man tries to pass on to someone always sounds like foolishness.”

Siddhartha by Hermann Hesse is a timeless philosophical novel that follows the spiritual journey of a young Indian man named Siddhartha during the time of Gautama Buddha. Dissatisfied with ritualistic teachings, Siddhartha leaves his comfortable life in search of deeper truth and enlightenment. His path takes him through experiences of pleasure, pain, love, and renunciation until he finally discovers peace within himself by understanding life’s unity.

⭐ Rating: 4.7 / 5 – A calm, reflective, and life-changing read.

Hermann Hesse’s writing is poetic and deeply human. Through simple storytelling, he explores the eternal quest for meaning — how true wisdom comes not from teachers or books but from one’s own experience. Readers seeking introspection and mindfulness will find *Siddhartha* soothing, philosophical, and meditative. A must-read for anyone exploring spirituality or inner peace.
