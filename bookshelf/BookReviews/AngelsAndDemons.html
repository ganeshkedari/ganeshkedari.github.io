Thriller & Mystery

Angels & Demons

Dan Brown

“Science and religion are not at odds. Science is simply too young to understand.”

Angels & Demons is the first book in Dan Brown’s Robert Langdon series. It opens with the shocking murder of a CERN scientist branded with a mysterious symbol — the mark of the secret Illuminati society. Harvard symbologist Robert Langdon joins forces with Italian scientist Vittoria Vetra to uncover a centuries-old conspiracy that threatens to destroy the Vatican during a papal conclave. The story races through ancient churches, crypts, and monuments of Rome in a quest full of codes, symbols, and intrigue.

⭐ Rating: 4.6 / 5 – A fast-paced, intellectual thriller blending art, science, and faith.

Dan Brown delivers a roller-coaster of suspense that combines history, technology, and religion in perfect balance. The narrative is rich with puzzles, art, and hidden meanings, keeping readers hooked till the stunning climax. Whether you’re a fan of historical mysteries or smart modern thrillers, Angels & Demons is an unmissable read that set the tone for Brown’s global success.
