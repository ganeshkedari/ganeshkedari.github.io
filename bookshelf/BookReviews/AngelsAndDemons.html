Angels & Demons

Dan Brown

4.6
Gripping Mystery

“Science tells us how the heavens go. Religion tells us how to go to heaven.”

Dan Brown

Angels & Demons introduces Robert Langdon, who is summoned to a Swiss research facility to analyze a mysterious symbol seared into the chest of a murdered physicist. He discovers the resurgence of an ancient brotherhood, the Illuminati, who have planted a bomb in the Vatican. Langdon must race against time to save the cardinals and the city.

This book is a fast-paced, intellectual thriller that hooks you from the first page. The mix of art history, symbology, and science is fascinating. Langdon is a compelling protagonist, and the setting of Rome adds to the atmosphere. It's a gripping read that makes you question everything.
