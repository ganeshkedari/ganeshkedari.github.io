Amazonia

James Rollins

4.5
Thrilling Ride

“Evolution is not a static event. It is a fluid, dynamic process.”

James Rollins

Amazonia takes you deep into the mysterious Amazon rainforest. A scientific expedition vanishes without a trace, only for one member to stumble out of the jungle years later, dying and strangely mutated. A new team, led by a former Special Forces soldier and a biologist, is sent to find out what happened. They discover a lost world filled with prehistoric creatures and a secret that could change the world.

Being a fan of adventure books, I picked this up and couldn't put it down. James Rollins writes in a way that makes you feel like you are in the jungle. The story moves very fast and has a lot of action. It reminds me of movies like Jurassic Park but with more mystery. The ending was a big surprise for me. I think everyone who likes thrillers will enjoy this book.
