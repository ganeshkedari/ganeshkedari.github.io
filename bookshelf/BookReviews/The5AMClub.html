Robin Sharma, The 5 AM Club

The 5 AM Club is a bestselling self-help book by Robin Sharma that presents a powerful morning routine to transform your life. Published in 2018, the book tells the story of an entrepreneur, an artist, and a billionaire mentor who guides them through the life-changing habit of waking up at 5 AM. Robin Sharma, a Canadian leadership expert and former lawyer, is the author of The Monk Who Sold His Ferrari and has coached many top performers worldwide. The book introduces the 20/20/20 formula, which divides the first hour of your day into three equal parts for exercise, reflection, and learning. Sharma combines storytelling with practical advice, scientific research, and ancient wisdom to show how early rising can lead to peak productivity, better health, and personal mastery. The book has inspired millions globally to join the 5 AM revolution.

I started reading this book during lockdown when my daily routine had gone completely out of control. The storytelling format made it easy to read, though some parts felt stretched. What worked for me was the 20/20/20 formula because it is simple and clear. Waking up at 5 AM is tough in the beginning, especially in Indian winters, but the book motivated me to at least try it. I liked how Sharma backs up his ideas with science and history rather than just motivational talk. The book does repeat the same points multiple times, which can feel boring. However, the core message is powerful. If you can follow even half of what the book suggests, you will see changes in your energy and focus. This is not a magic solution, but it gives you a solid framework to build better habits. Best read when you are ready to actually make changes, not just collect ideas.
