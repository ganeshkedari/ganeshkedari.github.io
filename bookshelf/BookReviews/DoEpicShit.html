Do Epic Shit

Ankur Warikoo

4.5
Relatable

"Failure is not a problem. Not learning from failure is."

Ankur Warikoo

Do Epic Shit is a collection of life lessons, career advice, and personal reflections from Ankur Warikoo, one of India's most popular content creators and entrepreneurs. The book is divided into sections on life, careers, money, and relationships, offering practical wisdom drawn from Warikoo's own successes and failures. From dropping out of his PhD to building and selling a startup, he shares honest stories and actionable advice without sugarcoating the hard realities of entrepreneurship and life. The writing style is conversational and direct, making complex ideas feel accessible. Packed with short, digestible chapters, the book is perfect for young Indians navigating career choices, financial decisions, and personal growth in the modern era.

This book feels like getting advice from a big brother who has been through it all. Ankur writes in a way that is very relatable to Indian millennials and Gen Z. His honesty about his failures, including his divorce and business mistakes, makes the advice feel real rather than preachy. I loved the sections on money management and time management because they are simple yet powerful. The short chapter format makes it easy to read in small bursts, and I found myself highlighting something on almost every page. Some people might find the language too casual, but I think that is exactly what makes it connect with younger readers. A must read for anyone in their 20s or 30s figuring out life.
