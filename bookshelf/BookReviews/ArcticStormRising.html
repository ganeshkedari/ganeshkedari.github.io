Arctic Storm Rising

Dale Brown

4.3
Cold War

“In the cold of the Arctic, the only heat comes from the fire of war.”

Dale Brown

Arctic Storm Rising takes place in the frozen north where tensions are rising. Russia is building a massive military base to control the region's resources. A small team of US Air Force officers and intelligence operatives must infiltrate the area to gather intelligence and prevent a global conflict. High-tech warfare meets the harsh reality of the frozen north.

Dale Brown is a master of the techno-thriller genre, and this book is no exception. The technical details are impressive, and the action sequences are intense. The setting adds a unique layer of danger to the story. It's a solid military thriller that keeps you guessing until the end.
