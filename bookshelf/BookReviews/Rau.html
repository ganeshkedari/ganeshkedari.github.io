"प्रेम मनाला जिंकते, पण कर्तव्य इतिहास लिहिते."

N.S. Inamdar, Rau

Rau is one of the most memorable Marathi historical novels, centered on Peshwa Bajirao and Mastani. N.S. Inamdar brings the era alive with political tension, courtly conflicts, and the emotional cost of choices made by people at the height of power. The writing feels cinematic without losing historical depth.

What makes this novel stand out is its balance: it is not only a love story, and not only a war chronicle. It explores loyalty, social prejudice, duty toward statecraft, and the loneliness of leadership. Bajirao is portrayed with both strength and vulnerability, while Mastani emerges as dignified, resilient, and unforgettable.

As a reading experience, Rau feels layered and mature. The language is rich, the pacing is engaging, and the emotional impact stays long after the final page. For readers interested in Marathi literature, historical fiction, or nuanced character-driven storytelling, this is a must-read classic.
