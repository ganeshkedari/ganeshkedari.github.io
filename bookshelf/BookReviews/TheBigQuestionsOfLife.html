The Big Questions of Life

Om Swami

4.5
Enlightening

"The purpose of life is not to be happy. It is to be useful, to be honorable, to be compassionate, to have it make some difference that you have lived and lived well."

Om Swami

The Big Questions of Life tackles profound existential questions that every person asks at some point: What is the meaning of life? Does God exist? What comes after death? Om Swami, a monk who gave up a successful business career to pursue spiritual enlightenment in the Himalayas, blends ancient Eastern wisdom with modern insights to provide thoughtful answers. The book is structured around questions readers commonly struggle with, offering practical spiritual guidance without being preachy. Swami writes with clarity and humor, making complex philosophical concepts accessible. His unique perspective comes from both his experience as a successful entrepreneur and as a mystic who has spent years in deep meditation.

This book feels like having a deep conversation with a wise friend over chai. Om Swami has a gift for explaining spiritual ideas without making them sound too religious or out of touch with reality. As an Indian reader, I connected with the Vedantic philosophy he shares, but he also includes examples from daily life that anyone can relate to. What I liked most is that he does not give you ready made answers but encourages you to think for yourself. The chapter on finding purpose really helped me during a confusing phase in my career. It is a short book but every page has something worth reflecting on.
